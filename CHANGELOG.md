# Changelog

User-facing changes by platform and version. See the [desktop releases](https://github.com/prompthunt/commit-standings-releases/releases) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/committed-commit-leaderboard/id6831797452) for availability.

## Desktop 0.1.52 (2026-11-21)

### New features

- Share the leaderboard, your race, your rank, or the top four from Mac. Choose a Square or Story image, then share it or save the PNG.

### Improvements

- See contribution streaks on the desktop leaderboard. Open someone's profile for a summary of your 30-day race.

### Bug fixes

- Keep people on the Windows board when one GitHub people source fails to load.

## iPhone 1.1.0

### New features

- Get pass, close-call, result, and record notifications while Committed is closed. Choose what to receive, quiet hours, and an evening summary in Settings.
- Pin up to three rivals to the top of your board. See how far apart you are and your weekly record against each one.
- See your personal contribution records and share a 30-day challenge as a Story image or link.
