# Changelog

User-facing changes by platform and version. See the [desktop releases](https://github.com/prompthunt/commit-standings-releases/releases) and the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/committed-commit-leaderboard/id6831797452) for availability.

## iPhone 1.1.4

### New features

- Finish 1st, 2nd or 3rd on a board of four or more and your summary card becomes a rank card with a gold, silver or bronze trophy, your rank, your Contributions and your streak.

### Improvements

- The summary card is simpler: your streak reads "12 day streak", and how recently the numbers refreshed is a small "5m ago" in the corner. The line showing how many Contributions it takes to pass the person ahead is gone.

## Desktop 0.1.54 (2026-11-23)

### Bug fixes

- On Mac, Committed no longer uses CPU while the menu is closed. The pulse on refreshing counts kept animating out of sight after a refresh finished.
- On Mac, counts no longer keep pulsing after a refresh is interrupted, for example by closing the menu mid-refresh. They go back to the last numbers.

## Desktop 0.1.53 (2026-11-20)

### Bug fixes

- A high daily contribution total alone no longer triggers the troll badge. The badge now looks for an unusually large increase between closely spaced refreshes.

## iPhone 1.1.3

### New features

- Share a face-off from anyone's profile: an image of you and them side by side on Today, 7 days or 30 days. It shows contributions only. Choose Square or Story, or switch to someone else on your board.

## iPhone 1.1.2

### Bug fixes

- A high daily contribution total alone no longer triggers the troll badge. The badge now looks for an unusually large increase between closely spaced refreshes.
- Dismiss the private-contributions tip from Your month or the Board. It stays hidden in both places.

## Desktop 0.1.52 (2026-11-20)

### New features

- Share the leaderboard, your race, your rank, or the top four from Mac. Choose a Square or Story image, then share it or save the PNG.

### Improvements

- See contribution streaks on the desktop leaderboard. Open someone's profile for a summary of your 30-day race.

### Bug fixes

- Keep people on the Windows board when one GitHub people source fails to load.

## iPhone 1.1.0

### New features

- Get pass, close-call, result, and record notifications while Committed is closed. Choose what to receive, quiet hours, and an evening summary in Settings.
- Pin up to three rivals to the top of your board. See how far apart you are and your weekly record against each one.
- See your personal contribution records and share a 30-day challenge as a Story image or link.
